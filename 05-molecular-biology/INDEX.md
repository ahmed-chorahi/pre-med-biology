# 05 — Molecular Biology

How the information in DNA is **copied, read, controlled, and occasionally corrupted** — the seven mechanisms that connect a sequence of four bases to the proteins that do every job in a cell. The section proceeds as the cell does: build the structure, duplicate it, make a message, process the message, translate it, control the flow, and repair the damage when control fails.

**What this section gives you that a diagram of the central dogma does not:** why the lagging strand must be made in pieces and what that costs the cell when telomeres shorten, why an error rate of 10⁻¹⁰ is a design specification rather than a lucky accident, how one genome produces the hundreds of cell types in one body, why bacteria can be killed by antibiotics that leave human cells untouched, and why a single base change causes sickle cell disease while thousands of other single base changes cause nothing at all.
