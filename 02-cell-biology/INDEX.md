# 02 — Cell Biology

Every organelle as **structure → location → function → mechanism → failure**. The organizing idea of the section is **compartmentalisation**: a eukaryotic cell is a set of sealed rooms doing incompatible jobs side by side.

**Prerequisites:** [prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells](../00-foundations/07-cells-prokaryotes-and-eukaryotes.md) from Foundations, and all of [01 — Biochemistry](../01-biochemistry/INDEX.md) — especially [proteins](../01-biochemistry/03-proteins.md), [nucleic acids](../01-biochemistry/04-nucleic-acids.md), and [enzymes](../01-biochemistry/05-enzymes.md).

**What this section gives you that a list of organelles does not:** why a lysosome's acidity is a safety mechanism rather than a detail, why a signal sequence decides a protein's entire fate, why one missing enzyme produces a storage disease while a missing address tag produces a different one entirely, why an antibiotic aimed at a bacterium can injure a mitochondrion, and why a defect in a single motor protein can rearrange your heart, your sinuses, and your chances of having children.
