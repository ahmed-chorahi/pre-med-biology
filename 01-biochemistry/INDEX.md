The four major organic macromolecules and the enzymes that act on them. The organizing idea of the whole section is **structure → function**.

**Prerequisites:** [chemical bonds](../00-foundations/04-chemical-bonds.md), [water](../00-foundations/05-water.md), and [acids, bases, and pH](../00-foundations/06-acids-bases-and-ph.md).

**What this section gives you that a formula list does not:** why an unsaturated fatty acid bends and a saturated one does not, why a denatured protein loses function, why DNA is double-stranded but RNA is not, and what an enzyme can and cannot change about a reaction.
