The structures from 02 put to work: **membranes and transport, energy conversion, and the cell cycle.** Two themes run through the section — **gradients** (built, spent, and defended) and **regulation** (every pathway and every transition has a control point).

**Prerequisites:** [02 — Cell Biology](../02-cell-biology/INDEX.md) — especially the [plasma membrane and nucleus](../02-cell-biology/02-plasma-membrane-and-nucleus.md) and [organelles](../02-cell-biology/03-protein-synthesis-and-trafficking-organelles.md) — plus [enzymes](../01-biochemistry/05-enzymes.md) from biochemistry.

**What this section gives you that a pathway diagram does not:** why a pump and a channel are different payment plans for the same problem, why fermentation makes no ATP and exists anyway, why cyanide kills by blocking oxygen use while a cell is drowning in it, why a tumour ferments with oxygen available, and why every statement in genetics about alleles separating rests on a checkpoint that counts kinetochores first.
